1. What the App does

The App creates GS1 barcodes — GS1 DataMatrix, GS1 Digital Link QR, GS1-128, EAN-13 and UPC-A — from data you enter, checks that data against GS1 rules, exports the codes as image and print files, and reads codes with the camera or from pasted text. It also reads and builds Chestny ZNAK (Честный знак) marking codes from their parts.

2. What the App does not do

  • It does not issue GTINs or company prefixes. These are licensed by GS1 member organizations, such as GS1 US.
  • It is not a certified barcode verifier under ISO/IEC 15415 or ISO/IEC 15416, and its checks do not replace verification of printed codes.
  • For Chestny ZNAK marking codes, it checks the structure only. It does not confirm that a code was issued, is authentic, or is valid for a product; only the operator of the national system can. The App does not connect to Chestny ZNAK or any other marking or traceability system.
  • It does not guarantee that a retailer, marketplace, or scanner will accept a code made with it.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Use only GTINs and company prefixes that you are licensed to use.
  • Print only Chestny ZNAK marking codes that were issued to you through the national system. The App reproduces the data you enter; it cannot make a code valid, and it must not be used to imitate codes you were not issued.
  • Test printed codes, and verify them with a professional verifier where required, before a production run. Check your retailers’ and printers’ requirements for size, placement, and print quality.
  • Use the App in line with the laws and the labeling rules that apply to you.
